## Practical task

This is the exercise the path is for. It needs no wallet and costs nothing. Pick
one real indexer and put it through the criteria properly.

<Checklist
  id="delegator-checkpoint"
  title="Evaluate one real indexer"
  items={[
    "Open Graph Explorer or Lodestar and pick an indexer that is not in the top three by size.",
    "Write down its self-stake and its current delegated stake. Compute how much headroom it has under the capacity multiplier.",
    "Find its indexing reward cut and query fee cut. Say out loud which side of the split you are on, to be sure you have the direction right.",
    "Find how much of its stake is currently allocated versus idle. This is the ratio that explains most of the difference between operators.",
    "Look for its parameter history. Have the cuts moved in the last six months?",
    "Search the community forum for the operator's name. Do they post under it, and do they answer questions?",
    "Put your intended amount and holding period into the calculator in the rewards lesson, and read the two right-hand figures rather than the two left-hand ones.",
    "Write one sentence saying why you would or would not delegate to this indexer. If the sentence contains only a rate, go back to the top."
  ]}
/>

## Before you commit anything

<Warn>
Nothing in this path is financial advice, and the site holds no live data. Confirm
every current figure on [Graph Explorer](https://thegraph.com/explorer) or a
dashboard before acting. Delegated capital is unavailable for
<Param name="undelegation_period_days" /> after you decide to leave, so treat the
decision as one you are making for a long period rather than a reversible one.
</Warn>
