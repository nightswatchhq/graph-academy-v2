No wallet needed. The aim is to make one real forecast and write it down, so that
in three months you can find out whether you were right.

### Make one real forecast

- Open Graph Explorer and find a subgraph you can identify a live application behind.
- Write down, in one sentence, why that data will be queried over the next year.
- Find the current signal on it, and the query fees it has generated.
- Compare those two numbers. Is the pool crowded relative to the revenue it produces?
- Check whether any indexer is currently allocated to it. If none is, ask yourself why.
- Find a second subgraph with similar fees and much less signal, and say which position you would prefer.
- Note today's date beside your one-sentence forecast, and set a reminder to check it in three months.
- If you could not write the sentence without using the word 'probably' twice, that is a finding too.


## Before you signal anything

<Warn>
Nothing here is financial advice and none of these figures are live. Confirm
current signal, fees and allocations on
[Graph Explorer](https://thegraph.com/explorer) or a dashboard before committing
capital. The deposit charge is burned on entry regardless of what happens
afterwards, so the research is the cheap part and it comes first.
</Warn>
