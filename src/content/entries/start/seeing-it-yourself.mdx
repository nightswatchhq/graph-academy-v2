Everything above is description. This is where you go and look at the thing itself.


Nothing here costs money or requires a wallet connection. The aim is to see that
the things you have been reading about are real, named, and countable.

### Go and look at the real network

- Open Graph Explorer and find the list of indexers. Note how many there are.
- Pick any indexer and find its self-stake, its delegated stake, and its indexing reward cut. Those three numbers are the whole delegator decision in miniature.
- Find a published subgraph and look at its signalled GRT. That number is curators betting on future query demand for it.
- Open the Academy parameters page and find one number where two official sources disagree.
- Decide which role you came here for, and open that collection.


## Where to go next

You have the shape of it. Where you go next depends on what you actually want to
do:

  **[Curators](/curators/)**.

- You want to consume data in an application: **[App builders](/consumers/)**.
- You want to understand how the rules get changed: **[Governance](/governance/)**.
